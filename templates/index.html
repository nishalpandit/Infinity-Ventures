Questions, answered.

How do I book a quick service?

Choose a service, pick a time slot, share your location and confirm. Your vendor arrives at the slot you chose.

How does posting a requirement work?

Select a category and type of work, enter your budget and location, and submit. Vendors near you send offers and you pick one.

Are the prices fixed?

Quick services show a fixed starting price. For big projects, vendors quote based on your budget and the scope of work.

Are vendors verified?

Yes. Every vendor goes through ID and skill checks before taking jobs on Sugu.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. You can change your booking from your dashboard before the vendor is on the way.
