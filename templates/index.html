How It Works

How Infinity Ventures Works

Choose between instant booking for immediate repairs or project bidding for major renovations.

Instant Booking Flow 4 steps · average completion in 90 seconds Fixed price
1 Select Service & Add-ons Browse the category or search by voice. Add-ons like gas refill, deep-foam wash or spare parts are priced upfront. Transparent rate card
2 Choose Date & Time Slot Pick today, tomorrow or any date up to 30 days ahead. Choose the morning, afternoon or evening shift that suits you. Free rescheduling
3 Verified Pro Dispatched with Live Tracking Get technician name, photo, rating and a live ETA map. OTP-verified doorstep check-in for your safety. Live arrival tracking
4 Inspect Work & Pay Securely Approve the completed job, then pay by UPI, card or netbanking. Money releases to the pro only after your sign-off. 30-day service warranty
Custom Project Bidding Flow 4 steps · 3–5 quotations in under an hour Best quote wins
1 Describe Requirement & Upload Photos/Videos Add scope of work, area or units, material preference, budget range and timeline. Attach site photos, floor plans or a walkthrough video. 2-minute posting
2 Local Verified Contractors Submit Quotations Individual technicians and registered contracting firms near you respond with detailed quotes including pricing, timeline, material brand and itemised work breakdown. Verified providers only
3 Compare Prices, Timelines & Chat Directly Side-by-side comparison table, past work portfolio, reviews and masked in-app chat. Negotiate line items without sharing your phone number. Call masking enabled
4 Award Contract & Pay via Milestone Escrow Break the contract into milestones. Funds stay in escrow and release on each verified stage: deposit, mid-work, finishing, and handover. Milestone escrow
